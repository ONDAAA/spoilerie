Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2025

What Spoilerie does

Spoilerie is a Chrome extension that detects potential spoilers in YouTube comments based on your current playback position. It does this by analyzing comment text against the video's transcript.

Data we collect

Spoilerie collects the minimum data necessary to function:

  • Comment text — visible YouTube comments are sent to our API for analysis. We do not store comment content after processing.
  • Video ID and playback position — used to fetch the transcript and determine spoiler relevance. Not linked to your identity.
  • Email address — only if you sign up for an account or join the waitlist.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your YouTube account information
  • Your browsing history
  • Your watch history beyond the current video
  • Any personal data from YouTube
  • Comments you write

How data is processed

Comment text and video metadata are sent to our backend API over HTTPS. The data is processed in-memory to classify spoilers and immediately discarded. We do not build user profiles, sell data, or share data with third parties.

Transcript caching

We cache video transcripts (publicly available subtitle data) on our servers to improve performance. This is public data provided by YouTube and contains no personal information.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (number of requests, error rates) to improve the service. No personally identifiable information is included.

Third-party services

  • YouTube — we access publicly available transcript data via YouTube's API.
  • Supabase — authentication and account management (if you create an account).
  • Stripe — payment processing for Pro subscriptions (if applicable).

Your rights

You can:

  • Disable or uninstall the extension at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

Changes
